01 · Overview

What is where.

The Brand book editor holds the colours, fonts, photos, lines and elements of The Cat & Gun. The Sticker editor, Print & feed and the Brand book take them from here.

  1. 1

    On the left stand the eight tools. Under «Brand editor»: «Overview», «Colours», «Fonts», «Photos», «Lines», «Logo & elements» and «Earlier versions». The tabs under the title lead there too.

  2. 2

    Lower down on the left: the Control panel, «View website», this guide and the short tour. The keys 1 to 8 open the tools.

  3. 3

    At the bottom the bar shows what is waiting, with «Before / after», «Save» and «Publish».

  4. 4

    On the phone the button «Sections» opens the list and closes after you choose.

Good to knowNothing you change reaches the other tools before you press «Publish».

02 · Colours

Change a colour.

All colours of the house stand in their palettes: Print, Super Juices and Website. Each card shows the name, what it is for and the value.

  1. 1

    Tap a colour. A window opens.

  2. 2

    Choose a colour from the palette of the house, or type the value: HEX like #A8232A, or R, G and B from 0 to 255.

  3. 3

    Change the name if you like. «Use this colour» takes it. «Hide» takes the colour out of use; it stays under «Hidden».

  4. 4

    «New colour» adds a colour to a palette.

Good to knowEvery element that wears this colour changes with it: change «Pool table red» and every sticker with that red follows.

03 · Colours

Pipette and colours from a photo.

You can also take a colour from what you see.

  1. 1

    «Pipette»: in Chrome and Edge it picks a colour anywhere on the screen. In Safari and Firefox it opens the photos of the house; tap a spot.

  2. 2

    «Colours from a photo»: choose a photo from your computer or phone. The main colours of the photo appear with their share.

  3. 3

    Tap one of them to take it, or «Offer as a new palette» to keep all of them as a new palette.

Good to knowA photo for colours may have up to 30 MB. It is only read in your browser and not uploaded.

04 · Colours

Palettes, shades and contrast.

Under «More options» you build palettes and check if text can be read.

  1. 1

    Switch «Mode» to «More options».

  2. 2

    «New palette» or «Edit palette»: give it a name and tick the colours that belong in it.

  3. 3

    In the colour window, «Tints and shades» shows four lighter and four darker steps. «Add all nine as a palette» keeps them.

  4. 4

    «Contrast check» shows the value for text in one colour on another: 4.5 for normal text, 3 for large titles.

Good to knowYour mode stays as you left it, on this computer.

05 · Fonts

Choose the fonts.

Four uses, each with one font: titles, running text, numbers and titles in print.

  1. 1

    Under «Which font for what», open the list next to a use and choose a font. The sample changes at once.

  2. 2

    Rename a font in its field. «Hide» takes a font out of use, as long as no use needs it.

  3. 3

    «Add a font» uploads a font file (WOFF2, WOFF, TTF or OTF, up to 6 MB).

  4. 4

    Under «More options» you choose which weights of a font may be used.

Good to knowThe fonts of the house are the same files as on the website and in the print pieces.

06 · Photos

Photos of the house.

The photos of the website, each with its title.

  1. 1

    Search with a word, for example «pool» or «juice». «In use», «Added» and «Hidden» narrow the list.

  2. 2

    Tap a photo to change its title, hide it or swap it for another one.

  3. 3

    «Add a photo» puts your own photo next to the others: JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 25 MB, at least 900 pixels on the long side.

Good to knowSwapping hides the old photo. It stays under «Hidden» and can come back.

07 · Lines

The lines of the house.

Name, place, opening hours and the sentences of the house, word for word as the design tools use them.

  1. 1

    Change the title or the text right in its field. Below it you see how many characters it has (5,000 is the most).

  2. 2

    «New line» adds a line. «Hide» takes it out of use.

  3. 3

    Under each line stands where it comes from.

Good to knowA line may not stay empty. Write something or hide it.

08 · Logo & elements

Every part in its own colour.

The wordmark, the ampersand, the pool ball and the stickers. Each colour in them is a part.

  1. 1

    Tap an element. On the right stand its parts, each with its colour of the house.

  2. 2

    Choose another colour of the house for a part. The picture changes at once.

  3. 3

    Under «More options», «Own value» gives a part a colour only in this element. «Back to the house colour» undoes it.

  4. 4

    Rename or hide an element in the same window.

Good to knowThe six dots in the Super Juices oval are six parts: each follows its own juice colour.

09 · Save and publish

Save, then publish.

Changes first wait in a draft. Publishing puts them into every tool.

  1. 1

    The bar at the bottom counts what is not saved yet. «Before / after» lists every change.

  2. 2

    «Save» keeps your changes in the draft. A note at the bottom says what was saved, with «Undo».

  3. 3

    «Publish» shows the list once more. Add a note if you like and confirm.

  4. 4

    If someone saved in another window in the meantime, the editor says so. Nothing is overwritten.

Good to knowWithout network your changes stay on the page. Try «Save» again once the network is back.

10 · Earlier versions

Bring an earlier version back.

Every save and every publication is kept.

  1. 1

    Open «Earlier versions» on the left.

  2. 2

    «Bring into draft» next to a version puts it back in the draft. «Original version» is the brand collection as it was built.

  3. 3

    Look at it, then publish. Or «Discard draft», and everything is as published.

Good to knowBringing back changes nothing in the other tools until you publish.

Help

When a note appears.

Each note in the editor has «What to do?». It leads here.

No connection

The server does not answer. Your changes stay on the page. Wait a moment and press «Save» again.

Someone saved in the meantime

Another window saved first. Your changes are still there. The editor shows what changed there; then choose «Save my changes» or «Discard my changes».

A field is empty

Names, titles and lines need at least one character. Write something or hide the piece.

Too long

A line holds up to 5,000 characters, a name up to 80. Shorten the text.

Not a colour

A colour value has a # and six signs, like #A8232A. R, G and B each take a number from 0 to 255.

Photo too large

A photo may have up to 25 MB, a photo for colours up to 30 MB. Make it smaller on your computer or phone and try again.

Photo too small

A photo for print and the website needs at least 900 pixels on the long side.

Not a photo

JPG, PNG and WebP work. Other files are refused.

Not a font

WOFF2, WOFF, TTF and OTF work, up to 6 MB.

File too large

The file is larger than the editor takes. Make it smaller and try again.

Too many requests

Many saves in a short time. Wait a minute and try again.

The saved data cannot be read

The editor keeps a backup. Reload the page. If the note stays, ask the person who set up the tools.

Not published

The new version could not be written. Everything is as before. Try again in a moment.

The request is not complete

The page sent something the editor cannot read. Reload the page and do the step again. Your saved draft stays.

Too many entries

A list holds up to 240 colours, 40 palettes, 30 fonts, 400 photos and 200 lines. Hide or remove some before you add more.

Two entries with the same key

Two pieces in one list carry the same key. Reload the page and do the step again.

A palette names a missing colour

A palette holds a colour that no longer exists. Open the palette and take the colour out, or add it again.

Font file missing

The file of a new font did not arrive. Upload the font again.

Font too large

A font file may have up to 6 MB.

Elements come from the print pieces

Logo, signs and stickers come from the print pieces of the house. They can be renamed, recoloured and hidden here. A new element cannot be added.

Photo not complete

A new photo did not arrive completely. Choose it again.

File not received

The file did not arrive or arrived damaged. Choose it again.

An uploaded file is gone

A photo or font of the draft is missing on the computer. Nothing was published. Upload the file again, save, then publish.

The new version did not pass the check

The editor checks a new version before it goes out. This one could not be written cleanly. Nothing was published. Try again in a moment.

Version not found

This earlier version is not in the list any more. Reload the page and choose from «Earlier versions» again.

The brand collection cannot be read

The file with the brand of the house (fundus.json) is missing or damaged. Ask the person who set up the tools.

Something went wrong

Reload the page. Your saved draft stays. If it happens again, ask the person who set up the tools.
