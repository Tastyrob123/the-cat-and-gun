01 · Overview

How the Brand book is built.

The Brand book is the brand of The Cat & Gun in one place. Three parts for every day, and the style guide to look things up.

  1. 1

    Under the title is a row of tabs: «Overview», «Photos», «Logo & files», «Colour check» and «Style guide».

  2. 2

    On the left are all tools of the house, and below them «Control panel», the guide and the short tour. At the bottom of every page are the guide, «View website», «Change» and all files of the brand at once.

  3. 3

    On the phone the list on the left is hidden. «Control panel» is at the top right.

Good to knowThe Brand book saves nothing. What you get lands on your computer. Everything it shows comes from the Brand book editor.

02 · Photos

Find a photo.

All photos of the house are under «Photos»: breakfast, drinks, the pool table, the garden, the market and the team.

  1. 1

    Type into the search field, for example «pool», «juice» or «bagel».

  2. 2

    Next to it, choose portrait or landscape.

  3. 3

    Above the photos it says how many fit. «Reset filters» shows all of them again.

Good to knowFor an Instagram post, portrait photos work best: choose «Portrait», then the least is cut away.

03 · Photos

Get the right size.

A click on a photo opens it large. On the right are four ready sizes.

  1. 1

    Point at «Instagram post» (1080 × 1350) or «Story» (1080 × 1920). The crop appears in the picture.

  2. 2

    Drag the frame to the part that should show. «Get» takes exactly this crop.

  3. 3

    «Website» gets the photo with 2000 pixels on the longest side, «Print» in full size. Two buttons open the photo in Print & feed.

Good to knowIf a photo is too small for a story, the file name says «upscaled».

04 · Logo & files

The wordmark for a designer.

Under «Logo & files» is everything a designer or a print shop needs.

  1. 1

    The wordmark and the ampersand, each in black for light grounds and in white for dark grounds.

  2. 2

    «SVG» stays sharp at any size. «PNG» has a clear background, for presentations and office programs.

  3. 3

    The herringbone pattern of the website comes as a tile. «All files (ZIP)» gets everything at once, with the fonts.

Good to knowThe files are made from the brand book as it is published. If a colour changes in the Brand book editor, the files change too.

05 · Logo & files

Copy colour values.

The colours stand with name and note: the print pieces, the six Super Juices and the website. One click is enough.

  1. 1

    Click a colour: the value (for example #A8232A for pool table red) is copied.

  2. 2

    A click on the RGB line copies the RGB values.

  3. 3

    Below, get the colours as CSS, JSON or ASE (for InDesign and Illustrator) and the fonts as WOFF2 or as one ZIP.

Good to knowEach colour says where it comes from, for example the cloth of the pool table.

06 · Colour check

Measure a draft.

When a designer sends a poster, a flyer or a post, check here if its colours fit The Cat & Gun.

  1. 1

    Drag the draft into the field or press «Choose file». JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 30 MB. For a PDF, save the first page as a picture.

  2. 2

    Press «Measure». The colours are measured here in the browser. Nothing is sent.

  3. 3

    Below the empty field there is always an example.

Good to knowThe check measures colours only. It does not judge pictures or words.

07 · Colour check

Read the result.

At the top, one sentence says how much of the draft is in the colours of the house. Below, colour by colour.

  1. 1

    Each colour of the draft stands next to the closest colour of the house, with its name and one sentence. Colours that are not house colours come first.

  2. 2

    The line on the left is red when a colour is not a house colour. Pure black is not a house colour.

  3. 3

    «Copy as text» gives you the result for an email to the designer. «Save result» puts it on your computer as a file.

Good to knowThe example was drawn for this guide. Its colours are really measured.

08 · Style guide

Look things up in the style guide.

The style guide shows the brand page by page: the house, wordmark, colours, fonts, stickers, photos, the lines of the house and the files.

  1. 1

    Press «Style guide» in the row of tabs or on the overview.

  2. 2

    Below the title of the style guide is a row of chapters. A click jumps to that page. «Save as PDF» at the top right saves it as a PDF.

Good to knowThe style guide always shows the brand book as it is published.

09 · Changes

Change the brand.

The Brand book only shows the brand. Colours, photos, fonts and lines are changed in the Brand book editor.

  1. 1

    Press «Change» at the bottom or «Change in the Brand book editor» above the photos.

  2. 2

    Save and publish there. The Brand book shows the new state right away in the same browser, otherwise within half a minute.

Good to knowEarlier versions are kept in the Brand book editor and can be brought back there.
