01 · Overview

How the tool is built.

This is where you see what guests ask the chat on the website when it has no answer, and where you give the chat new answers.

  1. At the top, how many guests left a contact and how many questions wait for an answer, the buttons «Reply now», «Try the chat» and «Publish», and on the right the chat as a guest sees it. Below: «Waiting for you», «How a question travels» and the areas of «What the chat knows».
  2. Under the big title, the six sections: «Overview», «Inbox», «Questions», «Your answers», «What the chat knows» and «How the chat works». The number on a section shows what waits for you. On the left, the tools of the house as in the control panel; on the phone, «Tools» opens the list and «Control panel» leads back.
  3. The bar at the bottom says whether changes wait in the draft. «Before, after» shows them, «Earlier versions» lists what was published before, «Publish» gives the chat its new answers.
02 · Inbox

Guests who want a reply.

When a guest sends a question from the chat and leaves a name, phone number, email or Instagram, it is in the inbox.

  1. Open «Inbox». At the top, «All», «New», «Open» and «Done». The inquiries are sorted by day (Today, Yesterday, weekday, Earlier). New ones are marked; opening one marks it as read.
  2. Click an inquiry. Below the list: the question in the guest’s words, the contact, when it was sent and from which page; on the right, the sheet for your reply.
  3. When everything is answered, press «Done». The inquiry stays in the list, paler, and «Open again» brings it back.
  4. Delete: inquiries contain names and contacts and are kept for twelve months. If a guest asks for it, open the inquiry, press «Delete for good» and confirm.
03 · Personal reply

A reply for one guest.

Under the question there is a sheet with the logo for a personal reply. Nothing is sent from here, and no mail program opens.

  1. Write the reply into the sheet, the way you would tell the guest.
  2. «Save reply» (or Ctrl+S, on a Mac ⌘S) keeps it with the inquiry. Leaving the page with unsaved text makes the browser ask first.
  3. «Copy text» puts the reply on the clipboard. Send it yourself by phone, message or Instagram.
Good to knowIf the same inquiry is open in two windows and both save, the second one gets the message «changed in another window». Nothing is overwritten; your text stays in the window.
04 · Questions

What guests want to know.

Every question guests sent from the chat, by topic: opening hours, food, drinks, the pool table, finding you, wifi and payment and more.

  1. Open «Questions». At the top the numbers: all questions, without answer, answers by you, with contact.
  2. «Without answer» shows only the questions that still need you.
  3. If a question does not fit the house, «Mark as done without answer». It stays in the list without the mark.
05 · Answer for every guest

Write the answer once.

What one guest asks, others ask too. Write the answer once; after «Publish» the chat gives it to everyone.

  1. At the question, press «Answer for every guest».
  2. Keep the question or make it shorter. Write the answer as you would say it on the phone.
  3. «Other ways guests ask»: words guests might use instead, with commas (for example: toilet, restroom). The chat already knows many words that mean the same and finds small typos.
  4. «Valid until»: for answers that only hold for a while. After that day the chat no longer says it. Leave it empty for no end.
  5. «Add to the draft». The question is then marked as answered.
06 · Publish

Give the chat its new answers.

Everything you save goes into the draft first. Guests see it only after «Publish». So nothing goes online by mistake.

  1. The bar at the bottom shows «Draft: n changes wait».
  2. «Before, after» lists each change: new, changed, removed or hidden, with the old and the new text.
  3. «Publish» opens a window with the list and a field for a note. «Publish now» writes the answers file of the chat again; the website pages stay as they are. The steps show while it runs.
  4. When it is done: «It is online.» If something goes wrong, the old answers file stays and the message says why.
07 · Earlier versions

Bring back what was before.

Every publishing is kept. You can put an earlier version back into the draft.

  1. In the bar at the bottom, «Earlier versions».
  2. Each version shows its date, the number of answers and your note. At the very bottom, «Original state»: the answers from the website without any of your changes.
  3. «Put in the draft» changes nothing on the website. Check with «Before, after», then «Publish».
08 · Your answers

What the chat knows from you.

Under «Your answers» are all answers you wrote, as cards.

  1. When something changes, press «Change» on the card.
  2. «Remove» takes it out of the draft; after «Publish» the chat no longer knows it, and the question comes back to you when a guest asks.
  3. At the top, «Add an answer» if you want to give the chat something before anyone asks.
09 · What the chat knows

Everything the chat knows about the house.

The chat only answers with what is here: lines from the website (opening hours, address, phone, menu, drinks, the pool table, the playground, events) and lines you added.

  1. On the left, the areas; on the right, the lines of the chosen area. Each line from the website shows where it is written.
  2. Change a line: tap it, change the text, «Save». The website itself stays as it is; only the chat says it differently after «Publish».
  3. Hide a line: tick «Hidden». The chat then does not say it. «As on the website» brings back the original line.
  4. Valid until: a date after which the chat no longer says the line, for example for a holiday time.
  5. Search: the field searches all areas. If nothing is found, you can write the answer right there.
  6. Test the chat: «Ask, like a guest» answers from the draft. This question is not saved.
10 · Something new

Add a new line.

For everything the chat did not know yet: a new event, a holiday closing, a special.

  1. Under «Something new», write a title the way a guest would ask, and the text with date, time and price if known.
  2. Pick the area. «Add a photo» if you like, for example the poster of an event. Large photos are made smaller in your browser; a photo under 600 px works but looks soft. Files that are not photos are refused with a message.
  3. «Add to the draft». After «Publish» the chat answers with it and shows the photo.
  4. The same works with «Add a line» at the top, where you can also set «Valid until» and words guests might use.
11 · How the chat works

From a question to an answer for everyone.

«How the chat works» shows the way of a question in eight steps, with the numbers of today.

  1. The chat on the website searches the answers file in the guest’s browser. There is no AI and no cost per question.
  2. The chat answers only when a line covers what the question is about. Everyday words such as «Sunday» or «serve» don’t count. A word no line knows, such as «Christmas» or «vegan», means no answer.
  3. If it has no answer, the guest can send the question to you. Name and contact are optional and marked that way.
  4. You write the answer once, press «Publish», and from then on every guest gets it.
12 · Try the chat

How a guest asks.

Here you see how the chat answers a guest, with your draft.

  1. «Try the chat» (in the big title of the overview, or on the left) opens a window: on the left four questions to tap, on the right the chat as a guest sees it on the phone.
  2. Without an answer it offers «Send to the house». Sent from here, the question lands in the inbox as an example.
  3. «The chat as on the website» on the left opens a test page with the chat as it will sit on the website. It uses the published answers.
