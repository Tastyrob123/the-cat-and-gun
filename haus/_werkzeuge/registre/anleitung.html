01 · Overview

How Visitors is built.

Visitors shows who visits your website: from which country and which website guests come, on which device, and what they look at. Here you only read. Nothing can be changed.

  1. 1

    The card at the top of each view holds the eight views in one row: «Overview», «Visits», «Origin», «Time», «Pages», «Trend», «On the way» and «How we count». One click switches the view.

  2. 2

    In the same card you pick the period: 7, 30 or 90 days. All numbers of the view count for this period.

  3. 3

    On the left the tools of the house, below them the way to all tools (Control panel), this guide and the «Short tour». «View website» is in the card at the top. The bar at the end of the page holds «Save as table»: it saves the visits of the period as a file for Excel, Numbers or Google Sheets.

  4. 4

    When Visitors opens, a small sign at the bottom right offers a «Short tour» for ten seconds. You can start the tour at any time with «Short tour» in the card at the top or on the left.

Good to knowThe pictures in this guide show test data. It is made up and marked as test data in the picture. Your real visits come from Cloudflare Web Analytics.

02 · Overview

Reading the overview.

At the top four numbers: visits (next to the period before), pages viewed, minutes per visit and how many guests were on the way, so called or opened the directions. Cloudflare counts the first two. For the other two the real view shows «–» and «not counted by Cloudflare». Below, the visits per day as columns.

  1. 1

    Move the mouse over a column: day and number of visits appear.

  2. 2

    «Where guests come from» and «Viewed most» show the first places. The link next to them opens the whole list.

  3. 3

    In the test data the last five visits follow below, one click opens the visit. The real view has no list of single visits, so this part is not there.

Good to knowCloudflare counts a visit when a page is opened and the guest did not come from another page of this website. Every further page of the same visit counts as a page viewed.

03 · Visits

Reading a visit.

«Visits» lists every single visit: on the left the list by day, on the right the chosen visit.

  1. 1

    Click a visit on the left. The picture shows the town the guest came from, the country sits small on it; next to it place, time, number of pages and length. The page stays where it is, only the card on the right changes.

  2. 2

    At the top right six details: when the guest arrived, how long they stayed, how many pages, which device, which language and which website brought them to you.

  3. 3

    Below, «The path through the website»: page by page in the order of the visit, with the time on each page. Under a page you see what the guest did there, like calling or opening the directions.

Good to knowA name never shows. The list of single visits is only in the test data: Cloudflare, where the real numbers come from, does not link the pages of one guest, so the real view shows the number of visits.

04 · Visits

Who was on the way.

Only in the test data: above the list of visits you narrow it down to all visits, only those on the way, only those with Instagram or TravelGO, only those who called, or only those on mobile. The real view has no list of single visits and Cloudflare does not count taps, so these filters are not there.

  1. 1

    In the test data, click «On the way». The list now shows only visits where the guest called or opened the directions. Their circle lights up.

  2. 2

    Open one of these visits and read the path: which pages did the guest look at before, for how long, and did they call or open the map?

  3. 3

    «Instagram or TravelGO» shows who opened the Instagram of the house or the delivery on TravelGO. «Called» shows who tapped the phone number, «On mobile» the visits from a mobile phone.

Good to knowIn the real view, «On the way» shows how often the Visit page with the map was opened.

05 · Origin

Where guests come from.

«Origin» shows how many come from the Philippines and from abroad, the countries, the website that brought the guest and the device. The test data also show places, how many come from Siargao and the browser language.

  1. 1

    «Countries» come from the connection of the guest. Cloudflare tells the country. Town and browser language are not counted. In the test data «Places» show the place of the internet connection, which can be some kilometres off.

  2. 2

    «Came from» shows which website brought the guest to you: Instagram, Google, Google Maps, Facebook or TravelGO. «Typed in directly» means: address typed in, a bookmark or a link from a message.

  3. 3

    «Device» tells mobile, tablet and computer apart. «Browser language» is only in the test data: the language the guest set in the browser (the website is in English).

Good to knowGuests who plan their trip to Siargao from abroad show up under «From abroad» and «Countries».

06 · Time

When guests drop by.

«Time» shows at which hour and on which weekday the visits start. Siargao time.

  1. 1

    At the top four peaks: when most guests come, the busiest day, how many from 6PM and how many on weekends.

  2. 2

    «At what time» shows 24 columns, one per hour. Move the mouse over one and the number appears.

  3. 3

    «Weekday and hour» puts both together: the brighter a field, the more visits.

Good to knowSo you see at which hours a post about a screening, the market or the menu reaches the most guests on the website.

07 · Pages

What is viewed most.

«Pages» lists every page of the website with its views. In the test data you also see the time on each page and how often it lay on the path of a guest who then called or opened the directions; Cloudflare does not count these two.

  1. 1

    The list is sorted by views, the most visited page on top, each with its picture.

  2. 2

    In the test data, «Average time» only counts while the page is in front. If it sits in a tab in the background, the time does not run.

  3. 3

    In the test data, «On the way» tells how often the page was seen in a visit that then led to a call or the directions.

Good to knowMany views and little time on a page usually mean guests only skim it. Few views and a lot of time mean guests read it once they find it.

08 · Trend

How visits develop.

«Trend» puts the chosen period next to the period before: is it going up or down?

  1. 1

    The columns show the visits per day, light behind them the same day in the period before.

  2. 2

    «Next to the period before» compares visits, pages viewed and pages per visit, with the difference in percent. The test data also compare time per visit, «On the way», Instagram and TravelGO, calls and directions.

  3. 3

    «Week by week» sums up Monday to Sunday, below the main countries as small lines.

Good to knowCompare whole weeks, above all after a new post or an event like a screening. Single days go up and down a lot.

09 · On the way

How often guests call or look up the way.

A guest is on the way when they tap Call or open the directions in Google Maps. Cloudflare does not count these taps. The real view says «Taps are not counted.» and shows how often the Visit page with the map was opened.

  1. 1

    «See the pages» opens «Pages» with the views of every page, Visit among them.

  2. 2

    Only in the test data: nine values show how often a visit ends in a call or the directions, from Home, from the Menu, by phone, the Visit page without a next step, from Playground or Pool & Bar, from Super Juices, call or Instagram, TravelGO and the directions. Each is a share in percent, below it the counted numbers.

  3. 3

    Also only in the test data: «Pages before the next step» and «From visit to call or directions», from the visit to the Visit page to the call or the directions.

Good to knowIn the test data only the tap on Call or Directions is counted. No phone number and no name is stored.

10 · Good to know

What is counted and what not.

The view «How we count» lists what is counted and what is never counted.

  1. 1

    Cloudflare Web Analytics counts the page, the time, the country, the device and where the guest came from (only the name of the website).

  2. 2

    Never counted: names, emails and anything a guest types. No cookies are set. The address of the connection is not used to follow a guest.

  3. 3

    Cloudflare does not link the pages of one guest. Town, language, time on a page and taps on Call, Directions, Instagram and TravelGO are therefore only in the test data.

Good to knowThe privacy note of the website should name Cloudflare Web Analytics before it is switched on.
