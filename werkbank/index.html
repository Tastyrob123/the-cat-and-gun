The website

Your neighbourhood hangout, on the phone.

Built for the phone first, with the house’s own photos, sentences and the full menu. Six pages: Home, Menu, Super Juices, Pool table, Playground, Visit. In the phone and the laptop you can scroll, both stay at the same place.

Home«Good mornings start here.» with the Bagel & Coffee Combo and Eggs your way, then the kitchen, the six Super Juices, the playground, the pool table, the beers on tap, the Sunday market and the three years.

MenuThe whole menu in four parts: Brekky from 7AM, kitchen, juices and coffee, and beer, with the prices in PHP.

Super JuicesThe nine juices on the menu, the coffee hot or iced, and the kombucha.

Pool tableThe pool table, the ten beers on draught, the espresso martini at the bar and the live screenings.

PlaygroundThe backyard with climbing wall and slide, and the Sunday Community Market.

VisitOpening hours, phone and «See you at The Cat & Gun!».
