Beer, wine and shot glasses

The wordmark with Catangnan etched into the pint glass, the ampersand on the wine glass and the small wordmark on the shot glasses.

Etched or sandblasted, so the mark stays through every wash. The product photos are stand-ins; the mark on them is the house’s own. The print file gives the three motifs in actual size.
