Box for food to go

A kraft box with the wordmark and «Your neighbourhood hangout» on the lid, and «See you at The Cat & Gun!» with the opening hours and the phone number on the front.

For burgers, curries and bagels to go and for the delivery orders. Printed in black and red on brown kraft; the print files give the lid and the front in actual size.

kraft meal box · lid print 180 × 120 mm · front print 180 × 50 mm

Paper bags

A kraft carrier bag with the wordmark and «Your neighbourhood hangout», and a small paper bag for the homemade bagels with the red ampersand and «Good mornings start here.»

The carrier bag for bigger orders and the Sunday Community Market, the bagel bag for the counter. The print files give each front in actual size.

carrier bag 260 × 350 mm, print 200 × 240 mm · bagel bag 140 × 240 mm, print 110 × 160 mm

Stamps

For plain bags, boxes, cups and napkins, in red or black ink. The print file gives both stamps in black, ready for the stamp maker.

Labels for sauces and seasoning
