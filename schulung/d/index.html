13 Schritte

Was gezeigt wird

  1. This is Inquiries & chat. Guests will ask the chat on the website, you give the answers. Here you see what they asked and what the chat knows. No AI.
  2. How a guest asks. The chat as guests will see it on the website. It answers from what you entered.
  3. No answer yet. When the chat has no answer, the guest can send the question to you, with a name and a way to reach them.
  4. What is new. New inquiries in the inbox, questions without an answer, and how many answers the chat has.
  5. What guests ask. Questions sorted by topic. Those marked «Example» are samples until real ones come in.
  6. Answer for every guest. Your answer, other ways guests ask it, and «Valid until». From then on the chat gives this answer.
  7. The inbox. Guests who left a contact. Each inquiry is new, open or done.
  8. A personal reply. Write it here, «Copy text», and send it yourself by WhatsApp, mail or Instagram. Nothing is sent from the tool.
  9. Your answers. Every answer you wrote, as a card. Change, hide or remove it here.
  10. What the chat knows. Everything the chat knows about the house, by area: opening hours, finding us, breakfast, food, drinks, pool and playground, events.
  11. Add a line. A title, the text, a photo and the area. «Valid until» for things that only count for a while.
  12. Test a question. Ask like a guest and see the answer before you publish.
  13. Publish and go back. New answers wait in the draft. «Publish» puts them in the chat, «Earlier versions» brings back an older state.
