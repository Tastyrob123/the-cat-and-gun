12 Schritte

Was gezeigt wird

  1. Your menu online. The menu page of the website, as guests see it.
  2. This is Menus & price lists. Drinks, coffee and juices, kitchen: one list makes the printed menu as a PDF and the menu page of the website.
  3. Three menus. Drinks, Coffee & juices and Kitchen, like the printed ones. A click opens one.
  4. The same place. Click an item in the list and the menu next to it jumps to the same spot.
  5. Change a price. The pencil opens the item: name, marks, price and «Show on the menu». On the right: how it will look.
  6. Saved in the draft. The PDF and the website stay as they are until you publish. «Undo» takes it back.
  7. Move, copy, hide. Every item can be moved up or down, copied, hidden or removed. «Add item» and «New section» add new lines.
  8. The website too. «Website» shows the menu page of the website with your draft.
  9. Design the menu. «Advanced»: click a part of the menu and change its font, size, colour, alignment and spacing. Parts can be dragged and made bigger.
  10. Before and after. What is in the PDF today next to the draft, new words marked.
  11. Publish. Five steps: check the draft, set and print the menu, compare PDF and data, build the menu page, switch to the new version.
  12. Earlier versions. Every published menu is kept. «Put in the draft» brings one back.
