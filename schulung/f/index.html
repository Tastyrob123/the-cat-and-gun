Was gezeigt wird

  1. Your brand book. Logo, colours, fonts, stickers, photos and lines of the house in one style guide.
  2. This is the Brand book. Here you take what you need: photos in the right size, the logo, the colours. Changes are made in the Brand book editor.
  3. What is here. Photos, files and the house colours, each with a way in.
  4. All photos. Search the photos of the house and filter by portrait or landscape.
  5. The right size. Post, story, website or print: the frame shows the crop, «Get» downloads it. «Design in Print & feed» opens it there.
  6. The logo as files. Wordmark and sign, dark and light, as SVG or PNG. «All files (ZIP)» takes everything at once.
  7. Colours as values. A click on a colour copies its value. All colours also come as CSS, JSON or ASE for designers.
  8. Check a design. Drop in a design, for example from the print shop, and press «Measure». Nothing is saved or sent.
  9. House colour or not. The result names each colour of the design: a house colour, close to one, or not a house colour.
