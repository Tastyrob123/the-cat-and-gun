02ColoursAll colours of the house stand in their palettes: Print, Super Juices and Website.7 functions
  1. Each card shows the name, what the colour is for and the value.
  2. Tap a colour. In the window, choose a colour of the house or type HEX (like #A8232A) or R, G and B from 0 to 255.
  3. «Use this colour» takes it. «Hide» takes the colour out of use; it stays under «Hidden».
  4. «New colour» adds a colour to a palette.
  5. Every element that wears a colour changes with it.
  6. «Pipette» picks a colour on the screen in Chrome and Edge; in Safari and Firefox it opens the photos of the house.
  7. «Colours from a photo» shows the main colours of a photo. «Offer as a new palette» keeps them. The photo is only read in your browser.

03More optionsSwitch «Mode» to «More options» for palettes, shades and contrast.4 functions
  1. «New palette» or «Edit palette»: give it a name and tick its colours.

08Save, publish, earlier versionsChanges wait in a draft. Publishing puts them into every tool.7 functions
  1. «Save» keeps your changes in the draft. A note shows what was saved, with «Undo».
  2. «Before / after» lists every change.
  3. «Publish» shows the list once more, takes an optional note and asks you to confirm.
  4. If someone saved in another window, the editor says so. Nothing is overwritten.
  5. «Earlier versions»: «Bring into draft» puts a version back in the draft. «Original version» is the brand collection as built.
  6. «Discard draft» brings back what is published.
  7. Each note has «What to do?», which leads to the help in the guide.

06Colour checkCheck if the colours of a draft from a designer fit The Cat & Gun.6 functions
  1. Drag the draft into the field or press «Choose file»: JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 30 MB. For a PDF, save the first page as a picture.
  2. Press «Measure». The colours are measured in the browser. Nothing is sent.
  3. One sentence says how much of the draft is in the colours of the house. Each colour stands next to the closest house colour.
  4. Colours that are not house colours come first, with a red line. Pure black is not a house colour.
  5. «Copy as text» gives the result for an email. «Save result» saves it as a file.
  6. The check measures colours only. It does not judge pictures or words.

07Style guide and changesThe style guide shows the brand page by page.4 functions
